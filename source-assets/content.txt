AI Education Website – Website Brief

Project Overview

Design and develop a premium, responsive website for Innovgeist Technologies Private Limited showcasing its AI Education Partnership initiative.

This website is not a workshop registration page or a coaching institute website.

Its purpose is to establish Innovgeist as a trusted AI Software Company and Education Technology Partner that helps educational institutions prepare students, faculty, and graduate learners for the AI era through practical, responsible, and industry-led AI education.

The website should reflect the quality of Microsoft Education, Google for Education, OpenAI, and Stripe.

---

Website Goal

The website should help visitors:

* Understand who Innovgeist is
* Learn about AI education programs
* Build trust in the company
* View institutional experience
* Learn about the trainer
* Contact Innovgeist for workshops and partnerships
